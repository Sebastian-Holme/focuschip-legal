Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026 · Norsk

Who we are

The data controller is MilesTech AS (Norway). Contact: post@milestech.no.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and an anonymous user ID.
  • License and chips: your license code and the IDs of the chips you pair.
  • Your answers: daily phone hours, hours spent on apps you get little value from, goals for the time you win back, and your preferred reminder time.
  • Focus sessions: start and end time, number of selected apps and estimated time saved.

What we do not do

  • We cannot see which apps you use or what you do in them. Your app selection stays on your device; Apple Screen Time performs the locking.
  • No tracking, no advertising, no third-party analytics.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use Screen Time data for anything other than running the app for you.

Purpose

To sign you in, activate your license, show your time-saved statistics and send reminders you asked for. Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database provider) in the EU/EEA and protected by access rules so you can only access your own data.

Your rights

You can view, change and delete your data at any time. Delete your account in the app (Settings → Slett konto) to permanently remove your account, license, chips, statistics and settings. You can also email post@milestech.no. You may complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Children

FocusChip is intended for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13.
